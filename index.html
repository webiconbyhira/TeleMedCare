About TeleCare

Why We Started TeleCare

TeleCare was founded with a simple mission: to make quality healthcare easy, fast, and accessible for every Pakistani family. We believe that finding and booking a trusted doctor should take minutes, not days.

Our platform connects you with verified, experienced specialists across multiple cities. Whether you need a heart checkup, skin consultation, or child care — TeleCare has you covered.
